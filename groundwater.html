Problem & Domain Context

Hydrogeological Consulting & Client Onboarding

Industrial plants, commercial complexes, and agricultural developments face stringent groundwater extraction regulations, Central Ground Water Authority (CGWA) compliance hurdles, and rainwater harvesting mandates.

Traditional consulting operations rely on offline spreadsheet calculations and friction-heavy manual inquiry intake. Global Groundwater Consultants addresses this by providing instant preliminary estimation tools directly on the web, backed by structured consultation booking workflows.

Core Technical Feature

5 Specialized Mathematical Calculators

Custom domain engineering calculators designed for environmental compliance and preliminary site feasibility.

1. Groundwater Recharge Calculator

Computes artificial recharge potential by modeling catchment surface area, average annual rainfall intensity, soil runoff coefficients, and infiltration pit dimensions.

2. Rainwater Harvesting Calculator

Calculates harvestable roof and paved surface runoff volume, sizing recommended storage tanks and de-silting chambers based on monsoon cycle distributions.

3. Water Requirement Calculator

Forecasts daily water demand across residential per-capita benchmarks, commercial occupancy, and industrial process consumption for CGWA compliance filings.

4. Project Cost Estimator

Calculates itemized budgetary costs for recharge shafts, injection wells, and filtration units, exporting downloadable client summary sheets using jsPDF.

5. Unit Conversion Tool

Instant bidirectional conversion across hydrogeological units: hydraulic conductivity (m/day, cm/s), volumetric discharge (LPS, GPM, m³/hr), and pressure heads.
